How buying works

  1. Ask first, if you want. Measurements, fabric, how something actually fits — message me and I will tell you straight, including when a piece is not right for you.
  2. Buy it on Vinted. Every piece links to its listing. Payment, postage and buyer protection are all handled there, so there is no separate checkout on this site and I never see your card details.
  3. It posts from Prague. Usually within a day or two of the order. Vinted's buyer protection covers the return if the item is not as described.

Everything is second-hand or one-of-a-kind from my own collection, so each piece exists once — when it is gone it is gone. Sizes are the label size; ask me for real measurements before you buy.
