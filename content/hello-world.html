This is the first note in a new shelf of the Diaries. Until now this journal has had two subjects: what I wear, and where I eat. Here is a third.

Mindfulness is the part of my life that visitors to this site ask about most and that I have written about least. It is also the part hardest to write honestly, because the vocabulary around it in Europe has been worn smooth — a word on a candle, a subscription in an app. What I mean by it is narrower and older: the practices I grew up around, done on ordinary weekdays, in a Prague flat, by someone with a job and a tram to catch.

What will land here

Short notes rather than doctrine. What a practice actually asks of you on a Tuesday morning. What survives the move from India to a European winter and what quietly does not. The questions people ask me after a class, which are usually more practical than spiritual — how long, how often, what if I cannot sit still.

There will be nothing here about becoming a better version of yourself. The traditions I learned from are not self-improvement schemes; they are older than the idea that a person is a project.

Where the rest of it lives

The teaching side — classes, Ayurvedic treatments, journeys to India — has its own home on this site, and it is a different kind of page: what is offered, and how to reach a person. This shelf is for the writing. If you want the practice rather than the notes, start there instead.

That is the whole of the first note. The next one will have something in it.
