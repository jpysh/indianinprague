Straight answers

The questions merchants ask first.

Who is legally responsible for my products in Europe?

We are. We are the EU Responsible Person named on every listing under the General Product Safety Regulation, we hold the technical file for ten years, and we are the importer of record on EU stock. Without an EU Responsible Person a non-EU seller cannot list at all, and marketplaces have been removing listings that lack one.

Do I give up my stock or my other channels?

No. You keep selling wherever you already sell. On consignment the goods stay yours until they sell.

Does my export rebate and GST refund stay with me?

Yes. You export under your own IEC and LUT, so the RoDTEP scrip is yours and goods clear at 0% IGST with no working capital locked at the border. The e-BRC is self-certified against the remittance on your own bank statement.

What if a product fails a compliance check?

It is not listed. We tell you what failed and what would fix it. We will not list it and hope, because the name on the listing is ours.

Who owns the customer?

We carry the demand, the payment and the returns, so the customer buys from us. You see your sales monthly, per product and per channel, with the cost of every order.

Why should I trust this?

Because the operator is the first merchant on it. Our own goods go into Europe on the same route, with the same files and the same delivery promise, and the numbers here are the ones we run on.
