Your money

What you are paid, and when.

The part most rails leave to a phone call. It is written down here because a maker cannot buy silver against a promise.

How your price is set

You quote a price FOB India, per piece, per material. We buy at that price. It does not move because a listing did well, and it does not move because a marketplace ran a promotion.

The European retail price is ours to set and hold in parity across every channel, because marketplace pricing rules punish a seller who undercuts their own listing. Your margin is fixed at the point you quote it, which is the opposite of a take-rate.

When the money moves

Two structures, and a founding merchant chooses. Consignment: the goods stay yours in EU stock and you are paid as they sell, monthly, against a statement that shows every order and its cost. FOB purchase: we buy the consignment at air handover and you are paid on dispatch from India, which is faster money and a thinner price.

Either way payment moves through an RBI-authorised cross-border channel that settles the next working day and issues the electronic foreign inward remittance advice automatically. Nothing sits in an intermediary account waiting to be reconciled by hand.

Your GST does not stay stuck

Exporting under the LUT route clears the goods at 0% IGST, so none of your working capital is locked at the border. The input tax you already paid on silver, cloth or brass is refunded in cash against your shipping bills.

The alternative, paying IGST and claiming it back, ties up 15 to 18% of the invoice for two to three months. We will not put you on it.

What happens to stock that does not sell

Nothing holds warehouse space past eight weeks without demonstrated sell-through. Three exits, and yours is agreed in writing before the first shipment rather than argued afterwards: buy-back at the price we paid, outlet liquidation at ten per cent below list, or a duty-free batch re-import to India under Notification 45/2017-Customs.

On the re-import we file the EDPMS reversal for you. An unreversed export entry is what puts an exporter on the caution list, and that is our paperwork failure to prevent, not yours to discover.

If a consolidation is too light to fly

Your goods move on the next fortnightly cycle, or go atelier-direct on a duty-paid express consignment, whichever reaches the customer sooner. What does not happen is your stock sitting at the consolidation point for a month while volume is found.

Working capital, and when it exists

Not yet. From 2027 the intention is trade-finance partners advancing against verified FOB value within two days of air handover, repaid from sales proceeds. That is a partner balance sheet and not ours, and it is listed here as a plan rather than an offer because it is not live.

Why now

Three rules changed. Two shut the door, one opens it.

€3 on every parcel, since July 2026

The EU’s customs reform put a per-item charge on low-value imports and ended the cheap-parcel model outright. A single posted parcel from India no longer competes on price.

Transitional until the EU Data Hub in 2028, with an import handling fee due by November 2026.

No EU person, no listing, since December 2024

The General Product Safety Regulation bars a non-EU seller without an EU-established Responsible Person. In June 2026 German and French authorities ran a joint sweep of Amazon listings missing compliance data and issued 14-day removal notices.

The single hardest requirement for a small Indian maker to satisfy alone, and the one Guldaar exists to absorb.

Zero duty on your category, from 2027

Under the India–EU trade agreement, textiles, jewellery, handicrafts and leather go to zero duty, with provisional application expected from late 2026.

Rules of origin have to be certified for every consignment. That is one more document per shipment, and it is one we raise, not you.

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Who is legally responsible for my products in Europe?

Guldaar is. The General Product Safety Regulation requires an EU-established Responsible Person for every consumer product sold to EU consumers, named on the listing, reachable by an authority, and holding a technical file for ten years. Guldaar holds that role for everything on the rail and is the importer of record on EU-held stock.

Under the 2026 EU customs reform Guldaar is also the deemed importer on goods shipped directly from the atelier, and as a marketplace operator it keeps trader-traceability records under Article 30 of the Digital Services Act.

Do I have to give up my stock or my other channels?

No. The contract is semi-managed: you own the goods the whole way, and Guldaar owns logistics, compliance, listing and demand. Keep selling wherever you already sell.

Commission on marketplace sales is zero, because the marketplace has already taken 8–15% and stacking a second cut is the model this was built against.

How does the money get back to India?

You export under your own IEC and LUT, so the export stays yours: the RoDTEP scrip is yours to claim, and the e-BRC is self-certified against the inward remittance shown on your own bank’s statement, as the DGFT framework provides for.

Guldaar buys FOB India and becomes the importer of record in Spain. Nothing sits in an intermediary account waiting to be reconciled.

What does it cost, exactly?

€29 a month at the founding rate, €79 at list. 0% commission on marketplace sales, 5% on sales made on guldaarbazaar.com, and logistics billed at cost + 10% with the underlying cost visible to you.

The own-store rate has a published path to 10%, and the condition is stated rather than reserved: it moves only once own-store sales sustain €25,000 a month for three consecutive months, and merchants who joined before that keep 5% for a further twelve months.

What happens if a product fails a compliance check?

It does not get listed. REACH certification is a hard gate before listing for jewellery and metalware, compliance is filed per product family rather than per item, and no family enters the catalogue without a file.

If a test fails we tell you what failed and what would fix it. What we will not do is list it anyway and hope, because the name on the listing is ours.

How fast does delivery actually reach a customer?

Two tiers. Bestsellers held as EU stock in Spain reach a Spanish address in 1–2 days and Germany, France or Italy in 2–4 days. Everything else ships from the atelier in 8–14 days, duty and VAT paid at checkout so nothing is demanded at the door.

A product earns its way from the second tier into the first on its own sales, reviewed weekly, and nothing holds warehouse space beyond eight weeks of demonstrated sell-through.

Who owns the customer relationship?

Guldaar carries the demand, the payment and the returns, so the customer transacts with Guldaar. You get your own sales visible to you monthly, per product and per channel, including the per-order cost that produced them.

If a channel loses money for you, you will see it in that statement before we do anything about it.

How the invoice works

You are invoiced in euro by a Spanish-established supplier, with the duty and the import VAT already settled at the border. Nothing is demanded from you or your courier on arrival.

Outside Spain the supply is an intra-community one: we invoice without VAT under the reverse charge and you account for it at your own rate against your VAT number, which you give us once. A Spanish shop is invoiced with Spanish VAT in the ordinary way.

Payment terms, plainly

The first order is paid before dispatch, by transfer or card. From the second order you have thirty days from the invoice date, once the VAT number checks out and one trade reference is given. No letter of credit is asked for at any point, which is the entire reason a small shop can buy Indian goods this way at all.

Sample packs are prepaid regardless. They are small enough that credit terms would cost us both more in administration than the order is worth, and the return on your first pack is free either way, so prepaying it risks you nothing.

If something arrives damaged or wrong

Tell us within fourteen days of delivery, with a photograph. You get a credit note or a replacement on the next dispatch, and where the fault is ours we pay the return freight.

On your first order, the sample pack, we pay it either way. Send back what does not suit the shop, for any reason or none, and we refund it. That is one pack per buyer, which we say here rather than in a footnote: it is there so trying us costs you nothing, not as a standing way to borrow stock. On later orders a return for preference is yours to fund.

Handmade goods vary. A variation in a block print or a hammer mark on brass is the product, not a defect, and we describe the range at the point of order rather than defending it afterwards.

Lead times, restocks and made-to-order

Stocked lines leave Spain the next working day: one to two days to a Spanish address, two to four to Germany, France and Italy. Made-to-order and personalised pieces are crafted in India in around three days and arrive on a duty-paid express consignment in five to seven.

Restocks follow the fortnightly consolidation, so a line that sells through in your shop is back with you inside three weeks rather than three months.
