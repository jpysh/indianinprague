Ever wondered what Nietzsche, Aristotle, Plato, Marx, Schopenhauer, John Stuart Mill or Simone de Beauvoir would make of modern life? This book offers a fun, thought-provoking crash course in philosophy without feeling like a dry academic textbook. “What Would Nietzsche Do? – Philosophical Solutions to Everyday Problems” by Marcus Weeks is a witty and accessible introduction to philosophy, using the ideas of some of history’s greatest thinkers to tackle real-life questions and everyday dilemmas.
