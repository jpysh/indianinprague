Four women left their regular lives behind for fifty six days to ride motorbikes 17,000 kilometres across six countries, from Hyderabad through India's northeast, Myanmar, Thailand, Laos and Vietnam along the Mekong River to Cambodia. Piya Bahadur recounts a once in a lifetime journey through terrain rarely seen by Indian travellers, and her own transformation from an inhibited working mother into a confident adventurer. Praised by Incredible India's Director General of Tourism as an exhilarating read. #motorcycletravel #womenwhotravel #adventurememoir
