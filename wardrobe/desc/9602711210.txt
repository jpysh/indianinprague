The 1848 pamphlet that Marx and Engels called the history of all society as a history of class struggle became one of the most influential political texts ever written. Commissioned by the Communist League, it lays out a sharp analysis of capitalism and the class conflict it creates, and became the founding document of a movement that reshaped the twentieth century.
