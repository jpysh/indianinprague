A forbidden love story between a Sunni boy and Shia girl unfolds against the backdrop of 1990s Kashmir, as war and political violence close in on their world. Shortlisted for the DSC Prize for South Asian Literature and longlisted for the Folio Prize, the Financial Times called it a work that will undoubtedly endure. From the author of the international bestseller The Collaborator, praised by Arundhati Roy as fiction that makes you listen #kashmirliterature #southasianfiction #warfiction
