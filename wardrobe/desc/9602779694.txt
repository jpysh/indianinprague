A groundbreaking, meticulously researched account of the 21 month suspension of Indian democracy under Indira Gandhi, called the best and most wide ranging account of the Emergency ever written. It exposes how Congressmen, communists, businessmen, the judiciary and the urban middle class all facilitated authoritarian rule, not just Gandhi and her son Sanjay. Winner of the Karwaan Prize. 3.75 stars on Goodreads. #indianhistory #politics #nonfiction
