Physicist detective Manabu Yukawa travels to a struggling seaside resort town for a mining conference, only to be pulled into a murder investigation when a guest is found dead at the base of the cliffs. Kirkus called it ingenious in working one twist after another in a case that seems absolutely twist proof, and The Times dubbed Higashino the Japanese Stieg Larsson. The third book in the internationally bestselling Detective Galileo series, later adapted into a hit Japanese film. 3.96 stars on Goodreads. #japanesecrimefiction #detectivegalileo #mysterythriller
