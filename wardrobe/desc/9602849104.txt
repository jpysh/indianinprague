An ordinary man is unwittingly drawn into a senseless murder on a sun drenched Algerian beach in this cornerstone of existentialist literature. Le Monde ranked it number one on its list of the 100 Books of the Century, and Camus explored through it what he called the nakedness of man faced with the absurd. This edition features Matthew Ward's acclaimed translation, praised by the Chicago Sun Times as making it our classic as well as France's. Camus won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1957. 4.03 stars on Goodreads. #existentialism #albertcamus #classicliterature
