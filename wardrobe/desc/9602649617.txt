A young woman is found hanging in a third-rate lodging house with a suicide note on the table, but a sharp-eyed constable spots details that don't add up. Scotland Yard's manhunt leads to Charles Hobart, a man with means, motive, and opportunity, until MP and amateur criminologist Scott Egerton sets out to prove his innocence and unmasks the real killer at the eleventh hour. Author Anthony Gilbert was the pen name of Lucy Malleson, a Detection Club member praised as unquestionably one of crime fiction's greats.
