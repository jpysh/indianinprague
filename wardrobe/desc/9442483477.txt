Eternal Knot Tibetan Buddhist Earrings. In sterling silver, which oxidises and is very easy to clean and maintain for life. One of the eight auspicious signs of Tibetan Buddhism, the eternal knot symbolizes the unity of wisdom and compassion, samsara and nirvana and illustrates the illusory and ever-changing nature of life. A very unique take on eternal knot design, handmade in India by an artist.
