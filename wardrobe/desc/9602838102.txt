A quietly devastating novel about a humble couple exiled by their own families for marrying without consent, whose fragile peace is shattered when a debt forces a reckoning with the past. Natsume Soseki called this his favorite among all his own novels, and Haruki Murakami has cited it as the book he identifies with most strongly. The New Statesman called it a masterpiece of taste and clarity. This NYRB edition offers a new, more accurate translation than earlier versions. 4.07 stars on Goodreads. #japaneseliterature #natsumesoseki #nyrbclassics
