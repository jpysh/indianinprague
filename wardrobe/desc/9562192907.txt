Recognized by UNESCO as part of Indonesia's intangible cultural heritage, Ulos can be worn as an accessory (shawl/scarf/skirt), hung on a wall, used as a table runner, or even framed for display. Ulos is more than a traditional textile. It is a sacred symbol of warmth, protection, and ancestral blessings within the Batak community. Woven by hand with meaningful colors and motifs, each piece carries deep philosophical values and accompanies life's most important moments, from birth to marriage and even farewell ceremonies.
