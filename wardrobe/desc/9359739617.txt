Interlinked short stories set in rural and urban Pakistan, orbiting the household of a wealthy feudal landowner and the servants, relatives, and hangers-on tied to his world. National Book Award finalist, William Dalrymple called it probably the best fiction written in English about Pakistan. Compared by critics to Chekhov and Turgenev for its restraint and psychological depth. Great pick for fans of literary fiction, South Asian writers, and linked short-story collections like Jhumpa Lahiri or Mohsin Hamid. (National Book Award Finalist)
