Everything you learned about human history is wrong and this international bestseller proves it. Called "revolutionary" by the Sunday Times, David Graeber and David Wengrow demolish the myths of Hobbes, Rousseau, Diamond, and Pinker with a jaw-dropping new account of how humans really organized society for 30,000 years before farming, cities, and states. Goodreads rating 4.35 from over 60,000 ratings. One reviewer called Graeber and Wengrow superheroes of human development scholarship, a Howard Zinn for world history. If you loved Sapiens or Guns, Germs, and Steel, this is the book that argues against them and wins. A must-read for anyone who thinks they know where civilization came from.
