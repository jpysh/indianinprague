How buying works

  1. Ask first, if you want. Measurements, fabric, how something actually fits — message me and I will tell you straight, including when a piece is not right for you.
  2. Second-hand pieces: buy them on Vinted. Every piece from my own collection links to its listing. Payment, postage and buyer protection are all handled there, so there is no separate checkout on this site and I never see your card details.
  3. New stock: order on WhatsApp. Cards marked with a shop name are new goods from a Prague supplier, not from my closet. Message me and I will confirm the price and postage before anything is paid.
  4. It posts from Prague. Second-hand pieces usually go within a day or two, and Vinted's buyer protection covers the return if the item is not as described. New stock takes 5-10 business days across Europe.

Pieces without a shop name are second-hand or one-of-a-kind from my own collection, so each exists once — when it is gone it is gone. Sizes are the label size; ask me for real measurements before you buy.
