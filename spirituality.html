MEDITATION & THE SEVEN CENTRESfelt, not merely explained

Something you feel, not an abstract idea.

At the centre of Indian spiritual practice is meditation. Regular practice is described across Indian traditions as working through the body's subtle energy centres, often mapped as seven chakras — each linked to both a physical function and a quality of character.
